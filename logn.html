        Equations & formulas
        

          

            
Log-space parameters

            
\(\mu =\)\(\ln\mu_g = \ln\bigl(\mu_a^2/\sqrt{\mu_a^2+\sigma_a^2}\bigr)\)

            
\(\sigma =\)\(\ln\sigma_g = \sqrt{\ln\bigl(1+\sigma_a^2/\mu_a^2\bigr)}\)

            
Central tendency

            
\(\mu_g =\)\(e^\mu\quad\) (geometric mean = median)

            
\(\mu_a =\)\(e^{\mu+\sigma^2/2}\)

            
mode \(=\)\(e^{\mu-\sigma^2}\)

            
Spread

            
\(\sigma_g =\)\(e^\sigma\)

            
\(\sigma_a =\)\(\mu_a\sqrt{e^{\sigma^2}-1}\)

            
\(\mathrm{CV} =\)\(\sqrt{e^{\sigma^2}-1}\)

            
EF \(=\)\(e^{1.645\,\sigma}\) (central 90% coverage)

            
Higher moments

            
skewness \(=\)\(\mathrm{CV}^3+3\,\mathrm{CV}\)

            
excess kurtosis \(=\)\(\mathrm{CV}^8+6\,\mathrm{CV}^6+15\,\mathrm{CV}^4+16\,\mathrm{CV}^2\)

            
entropy \(=\)\(\tfrac{1}{2}(1+\ln 2\pi\sigma^2)+\mu\)

            
Percentile inversion

            
\(x_p =\)\(\exp\bigl(\mu + \sigma\,\Phi^{-1}(p)\bigr)\)
