Privacy

kvotab.se is published by Kvot AB. Its tools run in your browser, on your own computer. The site has no user accounts, sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising.

Google Drive in the Data Browser

The Data Browser can open files from your Google Drive. This is what it does with them, and with the access you give it.

  • What it can reach. When you click Google Drive and then Choose files, Google asks whether kvotab.se may open the files you pick. If you agree, the page can open the files you pick in Google's file picker, and only those (the permission Google calls drive.file), as it can a file you open with it from Google Drive itself. It cannot list, read, change or delete anything else in your Drive, and it is not given your name, email address or other account details.
  • Google Drive's Open with menu. You can add the Data Browser to that menu, to open a file with it from Google Drive. Google then asks whether kvotab.se may add itself to your Google Drive (the permission Google calls drive.install): that puts the Data Browser in the menu, and reaches no file. When you open a file with it, Google Drive opens the Data Browser at an address that holds the file's ID and your Google account's ID. The page uses them only in its requests to Google, for that file, and takes them out of the address straight away.
  • What it does with them. Each file you pick is downloaded straight from Google into your browser and opened there, so that you can look at it, draw it and export from it, as with a file from your own disk. That is the only use made of it.
  • Where it is kept. In the page's memory while the page is open, and nowhere else: nothing is written to your disk unless you save an export yourself, and nothing is kept by Kvot AB. The access Google gives the page lasts an hour, is held in the page's memory only, and is gone when you close the tab.
  • Who else gets it. No one. The files and the access are not sent to Kvot AB or to anyone else: the site has no server that could receive them. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models.
  • Google's part. Google's sign-in and file picker are loaded from Google the first time you click Google Drive in a tab, or when Google Drive opens a file in the Data Browser, and Google's privacy policy applies to them.
  • Taking the access back. You can withdraw it at any time in your Google Account, under third-party connections. The Data Browser leaves Google Drive's Open with menu when you disconnect it from Drive, in Google Drive's settings under Manage apps.

Kvot AB's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other files in the Data Browser

Files you drop on the Data Browser or open with Add Files are read in your browser and are not uploaded. A file you open with URL is fetched by your browser from that address. A GitHub token you give it, for a file in a private repository, is kept for that tab only and is sent only to GitHub.

What your browser keeps

Settings such as the light or dark theme, saved chart presets and a tool's last inputs are kept in your browser's storage on your own device. Kvot AB never receives them, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

Other services your browser contacts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages: like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the addresses of the pages you load (GitHub's privacy statement). Pages load program libraries from public content delivery networks (jsDelivr, cdnjs, unpkg, jQuery's and Plotly's), one page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and some tools fetch from other services when you use them: the departure boards from SL, nuclear data from NNDC, the reference checker from your Zotero library when you connect it, the translator's open model from Hugging Face, and the office map from Jawg. Each of these receives your IP address and the request, as with any web request, under its own privacy policy.
