@ heatmap
Values as coloured cells, each labelled with its number.

From a matrix (with labels for its columns and rows), or from a table: how many rows fall in each pair of groups of two variables, or the mean (sum, median, max, min) of a colour variable in each. Missing cells are dark gray. Setting a property of the chart (`h.Title`, `h.ColorLimits`, `h.Colormap`, `h.CellLabelFormat`) draws it again.

# Syntax
h = heatmap(cdata)
    A matrix.
h = heatmap(xvalues, yvalues, cdata)
    With labels.
h = heatmap(tbl, xvar, yvar)
    Counts of a table's groups.
h = heatmap(tbl, xvar, yvar, 'ColorVariable', cvar, 'ColorMethod', 'mean')
    A statistic of a variable.
h = heatmap(..., 'Title', t, 'Colormap', cm, 'ColorLimits', [lo hi], 'CellLabelFormat', '%.2f')
    Its appearance.
# Examples
## Mean temperatures by month and site
T = table(categorical({'Lund'; 'Lund'; 'Umeå'; 'Umeå'; 'Lund'; 'Umeå'}), categorical({'Jan'; 'Jul'; 'Jan'; 'Jul'; 'Jan'; 'Jul'}), [1; 18; -9; 15; 3; 16], ...
    'VariableNames', {'Site', 'Month', 'Temp'});
h = heatmap(T, 'Month', 'Site', 'ColorVariable', 'Temp', 'ColorMethod', 'mean');
h.Title = 'Mean temperature';
h.ColorData

@ piechart, donutchart
Pie charts and donut charts, their wedges labelled.

Each wedge's share of the total, labelled by `'LabelStyle'`: `'percent'`, `'name'`, `'namepercent'`, `'data'`, `'namedata'`, `'datapercent'` or `'none'`; a categorical array (or a table's variable) is counted. The wedges start at `'StartAngle'` and run counterclockwise unless `'Direction'` says. A donut chart has a hole (`'InnerRadius'`, 0.6) with room for a label in the middle.

# Syntax
p = piechart(data, names)
    A pie chart.
p = piechart(tbl, var)
    Of a table's variable, counted.
p = piechart(..., 'LabelStyle', s, 'StartAngle', a, 'Direction', d)
    Labelled and placed.
d = donutchart(data, names, 'InnerRadius', r, 'CenterLabel', s)
    A donut chart.
# Examples
## Energy sources two ways
share = [40 30 17 9 4];
names = {'hydro', 'nuclear', 'wind', 'bio', 'other'};
subplot(1, 2, 1), piechart(share, names, 'LabelStyle', 'namepercent');
subplot(1, 2, 2), donutchart(share, names, 'CenterLabel', 'TWh');

@ stackedplot
Each variable in an axes of its own, stacked, sharing x.

For a table, each variable (or those named) in its axes, one above another, sharing the x-axis: a timetable's row times, or `'XVariable'`; for a matrix, each column. Variables of different scales stay readable side by side in time.

# Syntax
s = stackedplot(tbl, vars)
    A table's variables.
s = stackedplot(X, Y)
    A matrix's columns.
s = stackedplot(..., 'Title', t, 'DisplayLabels', labels)
    Titled and labelled.
# Examples
## A day of weather
TT = timetable(datetime(2026, 11, 5) + hours(0:23)', 5 + 4*sin(pi*(0:23)'/12), 1010 - (0:23)'/3, 3 + rand(24, 1), ...
    'VariableNames', {'Temp', 'Pressure', 'Wind'});
stackedplot(TT, 'Title', 'Station A');

@ polarhistogram
A histogram of angles in polar axes.

Angles in radians are binned over 0 to 2 pi (in nbins bins, at given edges, or of `'BinWidth'`), each bin drawn as a wedge out to its count (or `'probability'`, `'pdf'`); `'DisplayStyle', 'stairs'` draws the outline only. `h.Values` and `h.BinCounts` give the counts.

# Syntax
h = polarhistogram(theta, nbins)
    In nbins bins.
h = polarhistogram(theta, edges)
    At given edges.
h = polarhistogram(..., 'BinWidth', w, 'Normalization', 'probability', 'DisplayStyle', 'stairs')
    By a width, normalized, as an outline.
# Examples
## Two headings
theta = [pi/3 + 0.3*randn(200, 1); 4*pi/3 + 0.5*randn(100, 1)];
h = polarhistogram(theta, 24, 'FaceColor', [0.3 0.6 0.8]);
sum(h.BinCounts)
[~, k] = max(h.Values);
round(rad2deg(mean(h.BinEdges(k:k+1))))

@ wordcloud
Words drawn at sizes by how often they come, or by sizes given.

Text is split into words and counted (short common words left out), or words come with sizes, from arrays or a table; a categorical array is counted. The most frequent are drawn largest, the largest tenth in a highlight colour. `'MaxDisplayWords'` limits how many.

# Syntax
wc = wordcloud(words, sizeData)
    Words and their sizes.
wc = wordcloud(tbl, wordVar, sizeVar)
    From a table.
wc = wordcloud(str)
    From text, counted.
wc = wordcloud(..., 'MaxDisplayWords', n, 'Color', c, 'HighlightColor', c)
    Fewer, coloured.
# Examples
## A short text's words
str = "river water flows over stones; water carries sand, and the river carries water to the sea. sand and stones settle where the water slows.";
wc = wordcloud(str);
[wc.WordData; string(wc.SizeData)]
