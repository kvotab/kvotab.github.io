@ polyeig

@ gsvd
The generalized singular value decomposition of a pair of matrices.

`A = U*C*X'` and `B = V*S*X'` with `U` and `V` unitary, `C` and `S` nonnegative diagonal and `C'*C + S'*S = I`.
