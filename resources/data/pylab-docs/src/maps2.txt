@ projcrs, geocrs, projfwd, projinv
Coordinate reference systems by EPSG code, and latitude and longitude projected to x and y and back.

`projcrs(code)` is a projected system: the national grids SWEREF 99 TM (3006) and its local zones, UTM zones (326xx north, 327xx south, ETRS89 258xx), Web Mercator (3857), LAEA Europe (3035), the British National Grid (27700) and others; other codes are looked up with PROJ when it is installed. `geocrs(4326)` is a geographic one. `projfwd` and `projinv` convert with a `projcrs` or a map projection structure.

# Syntax
p = projcrs(code)
    A projected system.
g = geocrs(code)
    A geographic system.
[x, y] = projfwd(proj, lat, lon)
    Projected coordinates.
[lat, lon] = projinv(proj, x, y)
    Back.
# Examples
## Stockholm in SWEREF 99 TM and in UTM zone 33
> SWEREF 99 TM uses the projection of UTM zone 33, so the two give the same coordinates.
p = projcrs(3006);
p.Name
[x, y] = projfwd(p, 59.3294, 18.0686);
fprintf('SWEREF 99 TM: %.2f E, %.2f N\n', x, y)
[lat, lon] = projinv(p, x, y)
[xu, yu] = projfwd(projcrs(32633), 59.3294, 18.0686);
fprintf('UTM zone 33:  %.2f E, %.2f N\n', xu, yu)
# See also
geocrs axesm mfwdtran

@ axesm
Map axes with a projection: the frame, graticule and labels drawn in it, and data given in latitude and longitude.

The projection is a name: cylindrical (`'mercator'`, `'eqdcylin'`, `'miller'`, `'pcarree'`), conic (`'lambert'`, `'eqaconic'`, `'eqdconic'`), azimuthal (`'ortho'`, `'stereo'`, `'gnomonic'`, `'eqdazim'`, `'eqaazim'`), transverse (`'tranmerc'`, `'utm'`) and whole-world ones (`'robinson'`, `'mollweid'`, `'hammer'`, `'sinusoid'`, `'equalearth'`). Properties set the region (`MapLatLimit`, `MapLonLimit`), the `Origin`, the frame (`Frame`), the graticule (`Grid`, `MLineLocation`, `PLineLocation`) and its labels. `plotm`, `geoshow` and the other map functions draw into it.

# Syntax
axesm(projection)
    Map axes.
axesm(projection, Name, Value, ...)
    With properties.
# Examples
## The world in Robinson's projection, with a route
axesm('robinson', 'Frame', 'on', 'Grid', 'on')
[lat, lon] = track2(59.33, 18.07, 35.68, 139.69);
plotm(lat, lon, 'r', 'LineWidth', 1.5)
plotm([59.33 35.68], [18.07 139.69], 'ko', 'MarkerFaceColor', 'k')
## An orthographic view from above Scandinavia
axesm('ortho', 'Origin', [60 15 0], 'Frame', 'on', 'Grid', 'on')
[lat, lon] = track2(59.33, 18.07, 40.71, -74.01);
plotm(lat, lon, 'r', 'LineWidth', 1.5)

worldmap setm plotm framem gridm

    Written.
geotiffwrite(filename, A, R, 'CoordRefSysCode', code)
    With a coordinate system's EPSG code.
# Examples
